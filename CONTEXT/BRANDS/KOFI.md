## Voice and Tone

The brand voice should be:

- Confident
- Conversational
- Practical
- Educational
- Direct
- Curious
- Honest about limitations

Avoid:

- Corporate jargon
- Generic motivational advice
- Unverified claims
- Unnecessary filler
- Excessive emojis
- Artificial controversy
- Overly complicated explanations

## Writing Style

- Use UK English.
- Open with a strong hook.
- Focus on one central idea.
- Use short paragraphs.
- Include a concrete example where possible.
- Make the practical takeaway clear.
- End with one relevant call to action or question.
- Match length and formatting to the platform.
