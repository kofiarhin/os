# Tickets

Tickets convert an idea or request into one outcome, one finish line, and one reviewable change.

## Rules

- Use `TEMPLATE.md` for consequential implementation.
- Create one ticket per independently valuable outcome.
- Record the intended user, scope, exclusions, experience, constraints, acceptance criteria, verification, and human-review items.
- Link supporting customer evidence rather than copying unsupported assumptions into a ticket.
- Keep status precise: `proposed`, `awaiting-decision`, `approved`, `executing`, `blocked`, `failed`, `implemented`, `verified`, or `completed`.
- A material change to scope, architecture, permissions, risk, dependencies, or acceptance criteria invalidates approval.
- Do not mark a ticket completed until its required verification has been inspected and its review findings are resolved or disclosed.

## Naming

Use `NNN-short-outcome.md`, for example `002-verify-tiktok-content-loop.md`.
